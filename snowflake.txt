Where the Snowflake Account URL Comes From

When you sign up for Snowflake (trial or paid), you get an account URL in the welcome email or on the web interface.

It usually looks like this:

https://<account_identifier>.<region>.<cloud_provider>.snowflakecomputing.com


Examples:

https://xy12345.us-east-1.snowflakecomputing.com → AWS in US East

https://abcd1234.east-us-2.azure.snowflakecomputing.com → Azure in East US

https://pqrs5678.gcp-northamerica1.snowflakecomputing.com → GCP in North America

-What to Enter in Power BI

In Power BI Desktop, do not include https:// — only the domain part.

For example, if your Snowflake URL is:

https://xy12345.us-east-1.snowflakecomputing.com


Then in Power BI, enter:

xy12345.us-east-1.snowflakecomputing.com

-How to Find It

Option A — From Snowflake UI

Log in to Snowflake at https://app.snowflake.com

Look at the browser’s address bar.

Example: https://xy12345.us-east-1.snowflakecomputing.com/console

Your account URL is: xy12345.us-east-1.snowflakecomputing.com

Option B — From Welcome Email

Snowflake trial emails contain a line like:

Your account is ready at: https://xy12345.us-east-1.snowflakecomputing.com


Just copy the part after https://
